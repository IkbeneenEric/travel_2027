01 / THE BIG PICTURE

Built for the journey,
not just the postcard.

A compact Armenian road trip that puts the spectacular south first, with a day for Lake Sevan and Dilijan. Built for early April: big scenery without relying on high-altitude hiking passes.

What early April actually means

Expect cool or mild lowland afternoons, chilly evenings and possible mountain snow. Carry layers.

THE ROUTE, IN ONE LINE

Yerevan → Garni / Geghard → Noravank → Goris / Tatev → Yerevan → Lake Sevan / Dilijan → Yerevan

04 / ON THE ROAD

The details that matter.

A hire car or private driver is the practical choice for the south. Stay on main roads; avoid spontaneous border detours.

TRAVEL ADVICE · CHECK BEFORE BOOKING

Do not enter the Armenia–Azerbaijan border zone; check the official Dutch travel advisory and keep to the main southern road.

Official advisory ↗

Arrival

Fly into Yerevan (EVN). Pick up a rental car only when you leave the city; airport transfer by taxi or pre-booked car.

Southbound

Yerevan → Khor Virap → Noravank → Goris is a long but scenic sightseeing day. Set off early and avoid driving unfamiliar roads after dark.

Tatev day

Goris is the base. Confirm the Wings of Tatev cableway is operating for your actual date and check wind conditions.

Sevan + Dilijan

A day trip from Yerevan is feasible, but limit stops. Lake Sevan is high and can feel wintry even if the city is springlike.

Stay

4 nights Yerevan, 2 nights Goris; adjust for your actual inbound and outbound flight schedule.
