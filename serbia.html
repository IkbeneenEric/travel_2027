01 / THE BIG PICTURE

Built for the journey,
not just the postcard.

A nature-forward one-week loop from Belgrade through the west. The signature landscape is Uvac Canyon, with Tara forests and Mokra Gora as equally strong highlights.

What early April actually means

Early spring can be chilly and wet in the mountains. Higher viewpoints may be muddy or temporarily inaccessible.

THE ROUTE, IN ONE LINE

Belgrade → Novi Sad → Ovčar-Kablar → Zlatibor / Uvac → Mokra Gora / Tara → Belgrade

04 / ON THE ROAD

The details that matter.

A rental car is strongly recommended west of Belgrade. Plan one overnight return to Belgrade before the flight.

TRAVEL ADVICE · CHECK BEFORE BOOKING

Dutch travel advice is green, but public demonstrations and road closures can occur, especially in cities.

The main loop

Three base areas work well: Belgrade, Zlatibor / Zlatar, and Tara / Bajina Bašta.

Uvac in April

Aim for an accessible viewpoint above the meanders. Do not assume a boat tour or cave visit runs that week.

Tara viewpoint

Banjska Stena is beautiful but forest access can be muddy or snowy; get local advice before the drive or walk.

Mokra Gora

The Šargan Eight heritage railway has its own seasonal timetable. Treat it as a bonus unless confirmed.

Flight safety

Return to Belgrade on Day 6, not from the distant mountains on the flight morning.
