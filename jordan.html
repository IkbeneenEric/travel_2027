01 / THE BIG PICTURE

Built for the journey,
not just the postcard.

An ambitious but practical desert-and-history journey, pairing Amman and ancient Jerash with Petra, Wadi Rum and the Dead Sea. Designed to avoid the current orange-advice area around Aqaba.

What early April actually means

April is generally comfortable for sightseeing, but desert nights can be cold and sudden rain can affect wadis.

THE ROUTE, IN ONE LINE

Amman → Jerash / Ajloun → Madaba / Dead Sea → Dana → Petra → Wadi Rum → Amman

04 / ON THE ROAD

The details that matter.

A rental car or private driver is easiest. For Wadi Rum use the local camp transfer and guide; avoid driving into the desert in a normal hire car.

TRAVEL ADVICE · CHECK BEFORE BOOKING

IMPORTANT: The Dutch government currently designates Aqaba, Azraq, Mafraq and some border areas orange, and most other areas yellow. Regional tensions may cause sudden flight or route disruptions. Check before booking.

Official advisory ↗

Safety first

This is inspiration, not a booking recommendation: current regional security conditions are unpredictable. Re-check Dutch guidance and airline operations.

Where to fly

Arrive and depart Amman (AMM), not Aqaba. The proposed route does not require visiting Aqaba.

Petra

A full day is needed. Start at opening time and allow a lot of walking and climbing; tickets and Jordan Pass requirements should be checked.

Wadi Rum

Meet your camp operator at the agreed visitor centre / village and use their desert vehicle. Nights can be cold.

Flight-day warning

Wadi Rum → AMM is a long drive (roughly 4.5–5.5 hours). Only book a sufficiently late Day 7 flight, or move the final overnight to Amman.
