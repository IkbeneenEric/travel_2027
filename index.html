03 / YOUR MOST IMPORTANT LOGISTICS

Three days in the
Sichuan mountains.

Chengdu high-speed rail + private drivers + two nights at Conrad Jiuzhaigou. No rental car required.

12
SEPTEMBER 12 · HUANGLONG

Train → station pickup → Huanglong → Conrad

Take an early train from Chengdu East to Huanglongjiuzhai. A pre-booked driver meets you at the station, waits during your 4–5 hours exploring Huanglong and delivers you and your luggage to the Conrad.

Station GPS 32.745251, 103.615086 Night 1: Conrad
13
SEPTEMBER 13 · JIUZHAIGOU

Conrad → park → Conrad

Book morning and afternoon transfers with the Conrad concierge or a local driver. Spend the entire day in Jiuzhaigou National Park using the park buses and trails, then return to the same hotel.

Park entry separate Night 2: Conrad
14
SEPTEMBER 14 · RETURN

Conrad → station → Chengdu

Private car from the hotel to Huanglongjiuzhai Station, followed by a midday high-speed train back to Chengdu. Stay one final night in Chengdu before travelling to Chongqing.

Leave a rail buffer Night: Chengdu
